GymGrid Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

GymGrid doesn't collect any of your data. Everything you add stays on your iPhone.

What GymGrid stores, and where

GymGrid keeps your check-ins: the photos you add and the details you give them, such as the workout type and a note, along with the date and time you checked in. All of it is stored only in GymGrid's own storage on your iPhone.

There's no account and no server. GymGrid has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't send your check-ins, photos or anything else to the developer or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

  • GymGrid uses the camera only when you tap to take a check-in photo. You can turn camera access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under GymGrid.
  • Choosing a photo from your library goes through the iPhone's own photo picker. GymGrid receives only the photos you pick and has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Photos keep their original details, which can include where they were taken. GymGrid doesn't read or use those details, and like the photos themselves, they stay on your iPhone.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, iOS includes GymGrid's data in those backups, as it does for every app. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

This page

This policy is published on GitHub Pages. Opening it, including from the app's Settings, is a visit to GitHub, which may record it as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. GymGrid itself sends nothing.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your check-ins stay on your iPhone until you delete them. You can:

  • delete a single check-in from its detail screen,
  • delete everything in GymGrid's Settings, with Delete all data, or
  • delete the app, which removes all of its data from your iPhone. Backups made earlier keep their copy until they're replaced or deleted.

Children

GymGrid doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
