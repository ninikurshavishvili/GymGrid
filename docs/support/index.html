GymGrid Support

Answers to common questions, and how to get in touch.

How do I check in?

Tap Check in on the home screen, take a photo or choose one from your library, pick the kind of workout, and save. There's one check-in a day. To add more photos later, up to six, open the day and tap Add photo.

Can I change or delete a check-in?

Yes. Open it from Today on the home screen, from a green square in the grid, or from All check-ins. Tap Edit to change its photos, workout or note, or Delete check-in to remove it and its photos.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts the days in a row you've checked in. Until you check in today, it still counts the run up to yesterday, and the home screen reminds you to keep it going. A whole day without a check-in starts it again from zero.

The camera doesn't open

Camera access is probably turned off. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap GymGrid and turn on Camera, or tap Open Settings in the check-in screen. You can also choose a photo from your library instead.

Will my check-ins move to a new iPhone?

Your check-ins are stored on your iPhone, and iOS includes them in iCloud and computer backups. Restoring a backup on a new iPhone brings them with it. GymGrid doesn't sync between devices.

How do I delete all my data?

Tap the gear on the home screen, then Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored on your iPhone. The privacy policy explains what GymGrid keeps and where.

Contact

For anything else, or to report a problem, open an issue on GitHub. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details or photos.
